# Customer Management

## Overview

The Customer Management module (`/customers`) is the central interface for managing loyalty members, digital cards, and promotional loyalty settings.

## Navigation

To access Customer Management, click **Customer Management** in the sidebar.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The top of the page displays four key metrics:

- **Total Members:** Registered loyalty customers.
- **Active Cards:** Customers with digital cards currently active in the portal.
- **Inactive Cards:** Customers whose digital cards are suspended or disabled.
- **Members Redeemed Reward:** The number of members who have redeemed at least 1 free reward.

## Customer Records List

The main grid displays the customer records with the following columns:

- **Customer:** Shows the customer's name and email.
- **Card Number:** The unique digital loyalty card number.
- **Progress:** Visual progress bar of stamps earned versus required stamps.
- **Membership Date:** The date the customer joined the loyalty program.
- **Status:** Shows if the customer's card is **Active** or **Inactive**.
- **Actions:** Contains the **View** button.

## Searching and Filtering

You can filter the customer list using the controls above the grid:

- **Search:** Enter a name, email, or card number in the "Search name, email, or card number..." field.
- **Status Filter:** Select "All Statuses", "Active", or "Inactive" from the Status dropdown.

## How to View Customer Details

1. Open **Customer Management** from the sidebar.
2. Locate the customer in the list.
3. Click the **View** button in the Actions column.
4. The Customer Drawer will slide out from the right, displaying:
   - **Personal Information:** Customer Name, Email, Member Since, and Card Number.
   - **Digital Card:** Card Status with a toggle switch to activate or deactivate the member.
   - **Loyalty Progress:** Current Stamps (progress toward next reward), Next Reward, and Lifetime Purchases.
   - **Authentication:** A generated QR Code and Unique ID placeholder.
   - **Recent Loyalty Activity:** A timeline of recent actions (e.g., "Purchase Completed"). You can click **View all activities** to see the full history modal.

## Available Actions

The header provides the following actions:

- **Refresh:** Reloads the customer list.
- **Configuration:** Opens the Loyalty Program Configuration modal.
- **Export Customers:** Downloads the customer list as an Excel file based on your current search and filters.

## How to Configure Loyalty Settings

1. Click the **Configuration** button in the top right.
2. In the "Loyalty Program Configuration" modal, adjust the following:
   - **Required:** The number of stamps required for a reward.
   - **Reward:** Select the reward type (e.g., Free Drink, Free Coffee, Free Pastry, Discount).
   - **Quantity:** The quantity of the reward.
   - **Status:** Toggle the loyalty program to "Enabled" or "Disabled".
3. Click **Save changes**.

## How to Export Customers

1. Open **Customer Management**.
2. Search or filter the customer list as desired.
3. Click **Export Customers** in the header.
4. An Excel file containing the customer data will be generated and downloaded.

## Important Terminology

- **Customer:** A registered loyalty member in the Sheilz POS system.
- **Digital Card:** The unique digital loyalty card assigned to the customer.
- **Stamps:** Points or progress units earned by the customer towards a reward.
